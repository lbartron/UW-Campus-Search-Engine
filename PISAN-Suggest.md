The build step (`backend/build_index.py`) precomputes L2-normalized embeddings for ~all documents. That said, the AI is **less ambitious than the proposal claimed**. The proposal (DYOP Proposal.md, section 4) promised three layers: OpenAI `text-embedding-3-small` embeddings, Pinecone vector DB, and Claude RAG answer generation with query rewriting. The "adaptive scoring" via `query_hints.json` is hand-curated keyword lists, which is closer to classical IR than to AI. The embedded-search-engine angle still counts as meaningful AI embedding, but it is a smaller AI surface than the proposal promised.

- **Pin dependencies.** Run `pip freeze > requirements.lock.txt` or convert `requirements.txt` (currently unpinned, lines 1-7) to `package==version` form so the grader can reproduce your environment in May 2027.
- **Add the promised benchmark.** Create `benchmark/queries.json` with the 25 queries from `DYOP Proposal.md` section 5, plus a `benchmark/run.py` that prints retrieval@3. Even a partial implementation closes a major proposal gap.
- **Cross-platform setup in `README.md`.** Add the macOS/Linux variants (`python3 -m venv venv && source venv/bin/activate`) alongside the PowerShell commands at lines 14-17.
- **Tune the scoring constants.** The `+0.25` topic boost (`app.py` line 229) and `+1.0` temporal-topic boost (line 240) appear arbitrary. Run a small sweep against the benchmark and document the chosen values in a comment.
- **Refactor API routes under `/api`.** Currently `StaticFiles` is mounted on `/` (`app.py` line 309), which works only because of FastAPI's route ordering. Moving `/search` and `/status` to `/api/search` and `/api/status` is a small change that prevents future collisions.
- **Add a `tests/` directory.** Even three pytest cases (`test_query_hints_loads`, `test_search_returns_results_for_kane_hall`, `test_soon_filter_excludes_distant_events`) would demonstrate engineering hygiene and let you catch regressions when tweaking the scoring formula.

### AI Integration Enhancements

- **Add the Claude RAG layer the proposal promised.** Wrap the top-k retrieval in an Anthropic API call that generates a 2-3 sentence sourced answer ("The next comedy show on campus is X on Friday in Kane Hall — [source]"). This is the single highest-leverage change for the AI Integration rubric category and is straightforward given that retrieval already returns `title`, `snippet`, `source_url`, and `start`. Use prompt caching to control cost.
- **Add LLM-based query rewriting** for ambiguous queries, as promised in proposal section 4 layer 3. "somewhere quiet to study" → "quiet study spaces library commons" before retrieval. Measure retrieval@3 before/after on your benchmark to substantiate the claim.
- **Replace the hand-curated `query_hints.json` with learned intent classification.** A zero-shot classifier (or a small Claude call) over a fixed label set ("soon", "weekend", "csse", "wellness", etc.) would generalize beyond the hard-coded keyword list and let you justify the design as "AI-driven" rather than keyword-driven.
- **Try a stronger embedding model.** `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` (384-dim) is the fastest option but among the weakest in retrieval quality. `bge-small-en-v1.5` or `gte-small` are drop-in upgrades with the same SentenceTransformer API and meaningfully better retrieval, measurable on your benchmark.
- **Re-rank top-k with a cross-encoder.** Run the top 20 cosine matches through `cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2` and re-sort. Adds ~100ms but typically lifts retrieval@3 noticeably, and gives you a concrete second AI component to point to in the writeup.
